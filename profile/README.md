# Vinsetra

**Built around the way you work.**

Vinsetra is a software studio in Kyiv, Ukraine, founded by Ihor Bokatov. We build custom web apps and database-backed systems for the way your business works. We connect your existing tools and add automation and AI where they help your team handle the work.

## What we build

- **Web apps your team installs on a phone** — orders, bookings, work orders, inventory, time and invoicing, with roles and a record of who changed what.
- **Automations and integrations** — in Apps Script, n8n, Make or Zapier, to end copy and paste between Google Sheets, Gmail, forms, your CRM, NetSuite or accounting software.
- **AI, where it helps** — agents on ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, connected to the tools you already use. They read invoices, emails and PDFs into your sheets, sort and answer requests, draft replies and keep the CRM up to date. You decide what a person approves; AI never approves payments.
- **Fixes and takeovers** — scripts someone else started. We check what is worth keeping first, then fix, finish or replace it.

## How it grows

Some projects start with a spreadsheet or an Apps Script fix; others need a web app and a database from day one. One way a system can grow: your spreadsheet gets checks on what people type, then scripts for roles and approvals, then a web app on a real database when the sheet is not enough. The old file keeps working until the new system takes over.

## How we work

- First, one small fixed-price step, so you see it working.
- A small team of people works with AI agents, and the people answer for every result. The agents plan, write and test code, each in a defined role; a person reviews every change before it ships.
- You own the code, the data and the accounts. After launch we can stay on for support, on terms agreed in writing.
